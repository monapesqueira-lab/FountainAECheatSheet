Sourcing
Where candidates come from — and how to stop wasting budget finding them

It tracks which boards are actually getting you hires, automatically pauses spend when you have enough applicants, and uses AI to recommend where to post next. You stop wasting budget and start filling jobs faster."

Agents are AI teammates that do specific jobs 24/7. They're sold as add-ons to core products, or standalone where applicable.
A
Anna — AI Recruiter
Conducts screening interviews with candidates — automatically, day or night
Legacy: AI Rec
Say this
"Anna calls your candidates within minutes of them applying and conducts a real voice screening interview. She asks your questions, follows up on vague answers, and sends back a score, a transcript, and a list of qualifications. Most of these interviews happen between 9pm and 5am — your team wakes up with interviews already done."
  • Anna never makes a hiring decision — a human always reviews and decides. Non-negotiable and legally required.
  • Candidates can opt into Anna or choose a human recruiter — consent is always built in
  • Questions are written by your team — Anna does not generate them
  • She follows up when answers are too vague: "Can you give me a specific example?"
  • Bias checks run continuously; she doesn't have good days or bad days
  • Bilingual — conducts interviews in the candidate's preferred language
  • Video option available if you want to see the interaction
Selling Anna Standalone (into Workday, Dayforce, UKG, etc.)

Candidates apply through the customer's existing ATS as normal. Fountain catches them at the screening stage — Anna conducts the interview, and the results (score, transcript, qualifications) sync back into their existing ATS. Their team never has to leave their current system to see the output.

When to recommend full ATS instead: If the customer is also frustrated with their current ATS — slow, hard for managers to use, bad candidate experience — lead with the full platform. Standalone Anna is right when they're locked into a system they're happy with and just want to eliminate manual phone screens.

When a prospect asks about AI security, compliance, or bias — don't guess. Use these lines. Everything here is accurate and defensible.
Master line — memorize this
"Fountain is built to protect global brands. We use Constitutional AI to physically prevent illegal actions in regulated markets like NYC or the EU. We're ISO 42001 certified — the new gold standard specifically for AI systems — and every AI action is explainable with a human always in the loop. This is why the world's largest companies trust us over legacy vendors who've struggled with these regulations."
🏛
The Three Pillars
Pillar 1
Constitutional AI — Anthropic Partnership
Compliance is built into the DNA of the system — not added as a rule on top. The system physically prevents non-compliant actions before they happen. It doesn't flag them after. It stops them.

If a job description or interview question would violate NYC Local Law 144, California AI rules, or the EU AI Act — the system won't let it through. That's a hard stop, not a warning.
Pillar 2
ISO 42001 Certification
This is the specific global standard for AI Management Systems — not general IT security. SOC 2 is about data security broadly. ISO 42001 is about AI specifically: how it's built, tested, monitored, and controlled. We have it. Most competitors don't.
Pillar 3
Explainability & Human-in-the-Loop
No black box. Every AI output comes with a plain-language explanation. Example: "Anna gave this candidate a score of 2 because they stated they did not have the required certification."

Humans always make the final hiring decision. AI recommends. People decide. This is non-negotiable and built into every AI feature.

Common Questions & Answers
"How do you handle bias in AI hiring?" ▼
"We run regular third-party bias audits by independent firms. Anna's questions are written by your team — she doesn't generate them. We run bias checks on her responses continuously, and customers can review any interview at any time. Candidates can also opt out of AI and choose a human recruiter — that choice is always available."
"What about EEOC and OFCCP compliance?" ▼
"Fountain's AI is designed to comply with U.S. employment law, including EEOC guidelines. We track and log every AI interaction for audit purposes. We also support required adverse action processes."
"What happens if the AI makes a bad decision?" ▼
"The AI never makes a final decision — a human always does. The AI surfaces a recommendation; your team approves or overrides it. If there's ever a question about why the AI said something, we can show you the exact reasoning."
"Who owns our data?" ▼
"You do. Fountain is a processor — you're the controller. We only use your data to provide the service. We don't train models on your candidates' data. Retention periods are configurable by you."
"Is Fountain GDPR compliant?" ▼
"Yes. We align with GDPR: data minimization, purpose limitation, configurable retention, and full support for data subject access requests. See trust.fountain.com for our full compliance documentation."
"Are you ISO 42001 certified or just working toward it?" ▼
"We are certified. ISO 42001 is live and listed on trust.fountain.com. This is the global standard for AI Management Systems specifically — not general IT security."

Key Terms Glossary
Agentic AI
AI that takes actions — not just surfaces information. Cue doesn't just answer questions: it posts jobs, builds schedules, sends messages. Generative AI tells you what to do. Agentic AI does it.
Anna
Fountain's AI voice interviewer. Conducts screening calls, scores candidates, returns transcripts and qualifications. Can run standalone (bolted onto an existing ATS) or natively inside Fountain ATS.
Constitutional AI
Fountain's approach to AI compliance via our Anthropic partnership. Compliance is built into the DNA of the system — not added as a rule on top. The system physically prevents non-compliant actions.
Cue
Fountain's AI agent layer. Works across all products via conversational interface. Previously called "Q" or "Copilot." Always say "Cue" — never "Q."
EMMA
AI agent that helps new hires complete I-9/W-4 via inbound call. Restarts forms, sends magic links, answers FAQs so HR doesn't have to.
Hiring Goal
Target number of hires + deadline attached to an opening. Drives AI recommendations and automated spend management in Sourcing.
Human-in-the-Loop
Humans always make final decisions. AI screens and recommends — never decides alone. Non-negotiable across all Fountain AI products.
ISO 42001
The global certification standard specifically for AI Management Systems. More targeted than SOC 2 (general IT security). Fountain is certified. Most competitors are not.
Opening
An active job requisition in Fountain — the equivalent of a req or role you're actively hiring for.
Pool / CRM
Fountain's talent bench — historical applicants, former workers, referred candidates. The first place to look before spending on job boards.
SAM
AI voice agent for worker satisfaction check-ins. Proactive outreach at Day 1, 7, 30, 60, 90, and exit. Flags retention risks before people quit.
