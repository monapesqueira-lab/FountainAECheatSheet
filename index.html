Agents are AI teammates that do specific jobs 24/7. Sold as add-ons to core products, or standalone where applicable.
A
Anna — AI Recruiter
Conducts screening interviews automatically, day or night
Legacy: AI Rec
Say this
"Anna calls your candidates within minutes of applying and conducts a real voice screening interview. She asks your questions, follows up on vague answers, and sends back a score, transcript, and list of qualifications. Most interviews happen between 9pm and 5am — your team wakes up with interviews already done."
  • Anna never makes a hiring decision — a human always reviews and decides
  • Candidates can opt into Anna or choose a human recruiter — consent always built in
  • Questions are written by your team — Anna does not generate them
  • Bias checks run continuously; she doesn't have good days or bad days
  • Bilingual — conducts interviews in the candidate's preferred language
Selling Anna Standalone (into Workday, Dayforce, UKG)

Candidates apply through their existing ATS. Fountain catches them at screening — Anna interviews, results sync back. Team never leaves their current system.

Recommend full ATS instead if: customer is also frustrated with their current ATS.

When a prospect asks about AI security, compliance, or bias — don't guess. Use these lines.
Master line — memorize this
"Fountain is built to protect global brands. We use Constitutional AI to physically prevent illegal actions in regulated markets like NYC or the EU. We're ISO 42001 certified — the new gold standard specifically for AI systems — and every AI action is explainable with a human always in the loop."
The Three Pillars
Pillar 1
Constitutional AI — Anthropic Partnership
Compliance is built into the DNA of the system. The system physically prevents non-compliant actions before they happen — it doesn't flag them after, it stops them. NYC Local Law 144, California AI rules, EU AI Act — hard stops, not warnings.
Pillar 2
ISO 42001 Certification
The specific global standard for AI Management Systems. SOC 2 = general IT security. ISO 42001 = AI specifically. We have it. Most competitors don't.
Pillar 3
Explainability & Human-in-the-Loop
No black box. Every AI output has a plain-language explanation. Example: "Anna gave this candidate a score of 2 because they stated they did not have the required certification." Humans always make the final hiring decision.
Common Questions & Answers
"How do you handle bias in AI hiring?" ▼
"We run regular third-party bias audits. Anna's questions are written by your team. Candidates can always opt out of AI and choose a human recruiter."
"What about EEOC and OFCCP compliance?" ▼
"Fountain's AI is designed to comply with U.S. employment law including EEOC guidelines. We track and log every AI interaction for audit purposes."
"What happens if the AI makes a bad decision?" ▼
"The AI never makes a final decision — a human always does. Your team approves or overrides every recommendation."
"Who owns our data?" ▼
"You do. Fountain is a processor — you're the controller. We don't train models on your candidates' data."
"Is Fountain GDPR compliant?" ▼
"Yes. Data minimization, purpose limitation, configurable retention, full support for data subject access requests. See trust.fountain.com."
"Are you ISO 42001 certified or working toward it?" ▼
"We are certified. Listed on trust.fountain.com."
Don't Say / Do Say
❌ Don't say
"Our AI makes hiring decisions."
"The AI is unbiased."
"Q handles compliance automatically."
"We're working toward ISO 42001."
"Just like ChatGPT..."
✔ Do say
"Our AI recommends. Humans decide."
"We run regular third-party fairness audits."
"Constitutional AI prevents non-compliant actions — it's a hard stop."
"We are ISO 42001 certified."
"Cue is Fountain's own agentic AI."
